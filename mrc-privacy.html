Privacy Policy

Marble Race Creator
Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what data Marble Race Creator (the "Game") collects, why, and what your rights are. It applies to all versions of the Game: Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux and web (including CrazyGames).

Who we are

The Game is made by Krzysztof Topolski - Ugly Pixels, Tomaszkowice 414, 32-020 Wieliczka, Poland (the data controller). Contact: uglypixelsmedia@gmail.com.

What we collect

The Game has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or payment details. It shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

Gameplay analytics. The Game uses GameAnalytics to understand how the Game is played and to find bugs. GameAnalytics receives:

  • a random identifier for your installation or browser, and on Android possibly the device's advertising ID;
  • device and platform information (device model, operating system, browser, game version);
  • your IP address, used to estimate your country;
  • game events, such as sessions, races started and finished, features used, and error messages.

We use this data only in aggregate to improve the Game. We do not use it to identify you.

Community tracks. When you browse community tracks, the Game downloads them from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log your IP address when files are downloaded.

Discord (desktop only, optional). On Windows, macOS and Linux, if Discord is running, the Game can ask your permission to show what you are playing in your Discord status. This is handled by Discord under Discord's privacy policy. We do not receive your Discord data.

CrazyGames. When you play on CrazyGames, CrazyGames may store your game progress and tracks in its cloud save so you don't lose them, and it collects its own data under CrazyGames' privacy policy.

Data on your device. Your tracks, settings and progress are saved on your device or in your browser's storage. We do not receive them.

Why we process it (legal basis)

We process analytics data based on our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and improving it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers named above (GameAnalytics, GitHub, and when you use them, Discord and CrazyGames). These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed to analyse how the Game is used, in line with GameAnalytics' retention rules, and then deleted or kept in anonymous aggregate form.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Email us to use these rights. Because analytics data is tied only to a random identifier, please tell us your platform and roughly when you played so we can find it. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

To stop analytics on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On the web, clearing your browser's site data resets the random identifier.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. It does not ask for any personal information, and analytics is used only to improve the Game. If you are a parent and believe your child's data should be deleted, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
